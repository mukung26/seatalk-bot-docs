# Build an App

**Source:** https://open.seatalk.io/docs/build-an-app

After understanding the [app capabilities](/docs/understand_app_capability), it's time to introduce app development on SeaTalk Open Platform.

Although the actual coding happens in your own IDE, SeaTalk Open Platform provides tools and resources that ease your app creation and development process. 

## Create your App

For your app to be delivered to end-users and put into use, you need to create an app on SeaTalk Open Platform. Your app will be created as a Private App so that only employees within your organization can access it once it gets released. App settings and configurations are completely controlled by you and your organization administrator.

Once an app is created, SeaTalk Open Platform will generate an `App ID` and an `App Secret` for it. They are required for [authentication](/docs/get_app_access_token) when your app calls [Server APIs](/docs/api_development_guide).

## Find Development Resources

For each [app capability](/docs/understand_app_capability), we provide a solid foundation for you to develop the app that addresses the need of your team and organization:

- Server APIs: a series of Server Open APIs for you to establish a robust backend service to support your app's business logic

- Web SDKs: a series of Web SDKs to support your app with web-related capabilities to interact with SeaTalk
