# An Intro to SeaTalk Open Platform

**Source:** https://open.seatalk.io/docs/intro-to-seatalk-open-platform

SeaTalk is the "One-stop for Work" that smoothes out work communication and administrative tasks to facilitate collaboration and employee management. SeaTalk Open Platform further empowers you to customize, automate and mobilize your workplace by supporting you to develop your own in-SeaTalk applications and services. 

With a wide range of [open capabilities](/docs/understand_app_capability) (e.g., APIs, SDK tools and common capabilities) and [smooth processes](/docs/become_an_app_developer), SeaTalk Open Platform helps you to serve various business scenarios and improve teams' work efficiency.

## What You Can Achieve with SeaTalk Open Platform

- Reduce communication costs to boost workplace efficiency: integrate your in-SeaTalk app with SeaTalk's native chatting functions to send messages and notifications to your end-users.

- Build advanced and customized admin services with SeaTalk Admin System integration: get the core features in SeaTalk Admin System, such as Organisation Management, Attendance, Leave, and Claim to create your own app with data retrieved via many server APIs provided.

- Let your employees complete work all in one place: convert your own office systems to in-SeaTalk apps so that all your workflows are now in the hands of your employees within this one-stop for work.

## How SeaTalk Open Platform Will Help You

- SDKs are provided for you to integrate SeaTalk functions into your app. Single-sign-on (SSO) is also provided for authentication and centralized account management.

- Server APIs are provided for your app's backend to perform various actions, such as retrieving data from SeaTalk Admin System and sending messages to your end-users via SeaTalk.

- Common capabilities are provided for your app to have more flexibility in implementing different business logic and delivering a better user experience.

- SeaTalk Open Platform Portal, which is the website you are looking at right now, will support you throughout the whole development process as a centralized app management platform with comprehensive documentation.
